Kata / Frasa : Makna / Penggunaan
Contoh Kalimat

**Blimey!** : Ekspresi kaget / kagum / heran
"Blimey! Didn't think that old thumper could still run!"

**Ton** : 100 mph (~160 km/jam) — target café racer klasik
"Hit the ton last night down the bypass what a rush!"

**Throttle** : Gas motor; gaya hidup cepat
"Ease up on the throttle, bruv, or she'll throw you off."

**Kickstart** : Nyalain motor pakai engkol kaki
"Had to kickstart her twice stubborn old girl."

**Rev** : Geber mesin
"Gave it a good rev before blastin' off the line."

**Lid** : Helm (slang biker)
"Don't forget your lid, mate you ain't invincible."

**Thumper** : Motor satu silinder
"That old thumper still packs a punch on low gear."

**Scrambler** : Motor off road klasik
"Took my scrambler through the woods mud everywhere."

**Custom** : Motor modifikasi sendiri
"She's a full custom built her meself, top to bottom."

**Built not bought** : Moto: bikin sendiri, bukan beli jadi
"This ride? Built not bought, bruv. Every bolt, my hands."

**Flat out** : Ngebut total
"Went flat out on the motorway felt like flying."

**Old school** : Gaya klasik, orisinal
"Ain't modern, but I like it old school. Raw and real."

**Twist the wrist** : Ngebut / tarik gas
"Just twist the wrist and let her scream."

**Ragged** : Kasar tapi berjiwa
"She's a bit ragged, but she's got soul."

**No frills** : Tanpa hiasan, fungsional
"Stripped her down no frills, just speed."

**Greaser** : Julukan biker retro
"He's a proper greaser—rolled up jeans and pomade slick."

Cafe to cafe : Gaya hidup ngebut antar kafe
"Been doin' the cafe to cafe run since I was seventeen."

**Torque** : Torsi / daya dorong mesin
"What she lacks in top speed, she makes up for in torque."

**Pipe** : Knalpot
"Swapped out the pipe now she roars like a beast."

**Ton-up boy** : Biker yang tembus 100 mph
"Back in the day, I was a real ton-up boy—Ace Cafe legend."

**Ace Cafe** : Kafe legendaris di London, ikon budaya caferacer
"Every proper ton-up boy knows the Ace Cafe."

**Rocker** : Julukan untuk biker caferacer era 50-60an (dari musik rock and roll)
"My grandad was a rocker, always talked about the old days on his Triumph."

**Mod** : Subkultur rival rocker, naik skuter (vespa/lambretta), pakai parka
"You got your rockers and your mods, never quite mixed, did they?"

**Greased Lightning** : Ungkapan untuk kecepatan luar biasa
"That Norton of his is greased lightning, leaves everyone in the dust!"

**The Ride** : Motor kesayangan
"Fancy a spin on the ride this afternoon?"

**Two-Wheeled Thunder** : Julukan untuk motor yang bertenaga dan bersuara keras
"When he revs that engine, it's two-wheeled thunder!"

**Custom Build** : Motor yang dimodifikasi secara personal dari nol atau sebagian besar
"He spent three years on that custom build, truly a work of art."

**Stripped Down** : Motor yang dihilangkan komponen tidak penting demi kecepatan
"I stripped down my bike, no fancy bits, just pure performance."

**Clip-ons** : Stang jepit, posisi rendah khas caferacer
"Fitted some new clip-ons, now I'm really tucked in."

**Rear Sets** : Footpeg mundur, posisi kaki lebih ke belakang untuk aerodinamika
"With these rear sets, I can really lean into the corners."

**Hooligan** : Biker yang berkendara agresif dan sedikit sembrono
"Watch out for that bloke, he rides like a proper hooligan."

**Brass Tacks** : Kembali ke dasar, hal-hal penting
"Alright, let's get down to brass tacks: how fast can she really go?"

**Spit and Polish** : Membersihkan dan memoles dengan sangat teliti
"Gave the old girl a good spit and polish before the show."

**Rubber** : Ban motor
"Need some new rubber before I hit the track again."

**Burn Rubber** : Mempercepat mendadak hingga ban berasap; ngebut
"He just loves to burn rubber every time he leaves the pub."

**Track Day** : Hari di mana biker bisa mengendarai motor di sirkuit
"Thinking of taking the Thruxton out for a track day next month."

**Bespoke** : Dibuat khusus sesuai pesanan
"That leather jacket is bespoke, fitted perfectly."

**Aces** : Sempurna, hebat
"Your new exhaust sounds aces, mate!"

**Skid Lid** : Helm (slang lain)
"Don't forget your skid lid, safety first, even for us."

**Get Your Knees Down** : Istilah untuk menikung sangat rebah hingga lutut menyentuh aspal
"He was really getting his knees down on those bends."

**Chasing the Ton** : Mencoba mencapai kecepatan 100 mph
"Every weekend, we'd be out chasing the ton on the bypass."

**Ride or Die** : Kesetiaan ekstrem pada gaya hidup atau teman
"This is my crew, ride or die, no questions asked."

**Back Road Blaster** : Motor yang cocok untuk ngebut di jalanan kecil/desa
"This old BSA is a real back road blaster, perfect for country lanes."

**Street Cred** : Reputasi di jalanan
"That vintage Norton definitely gives him some serious street cred."

**The Open Road** : Jalanan bebas, tanpa batas, simbol kebebasan
"Nothing beats the feeling of the open road and a full tank."

**Keep the Rubber Side Down** : Hati-hati di jalan (semoga tidak jatuh)
"Alright, mate, safe journey. Keep the rubber side down!"

**Full Throttle** : Gas penuh, kecepatan maksimal
"He went full throttle as soon as the light turned green."

**Wind in My Hair (Helmet)** : Perasaan kebebasan saat berkendara
"Even with a lid on, I can feel the wind in my helmet, pure bliss."

**Bespoke Build** : Sama seperti Custom Build, menekankan detail dan personalisasi tinggi
"This truly is a bespoke build, designed just for him."

**Flick it** : Mengganti persneling dengan cepat
"Just flick it into fifth and let her fly."

**Kick-Ass** : Sangat bagus, keren
"That new paint job looks kick-ass on your Bonneville!"

**Thruxton** : Model motor Triumph, sangat populer di kalangan caferacer
"My dream bike is a Triumph Thruxton, classic lines."

**Bonneville** : Model motor Triumph klasik lain
"He still rides his grandad's old Bonneville, runs like a dream."

**Norton Commando** : Model motor Norton yang ikonik
"The Norton Commando is a beast, truly a legendary machine."

**BSA Gold Star** : Model motor BSA yang terkenal dengan performanya
"You don't see many BSA Gold Stars on the road anymore, rare beauty."

**Matchless** : Merek motor British klasik
"My first proper bike was a Matchless, taught me a lot."

**AJS** : Merek motor British klasik
"He's got a lovely old AJS in his garage, pristine condition."

**Back Lane Bender** : Seseorang yang suka ngebut di jalanan kecil dan berkelok
"We're all back lane benders, always looking for the twisty roads."

**Get the Beans On** : Ngebut, mempercepat (slang)
"Come on, get the beans on, we're losing daylight!"
